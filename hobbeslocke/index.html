DBQ Essay Prompts & Teacher Discussion Guide

Use these questions for classroom discussion or student essay scaffolding:

Primary Prompt

To what extent was the Declaration of Independence a direct application of Lockean social contract theory?

Look closely at Locke's definition of "consent" and the "Right of Revolution" when legislators destroy property or exercise arbitrary power.

Textual Shift Analysis

Why did Jefferson substitute "the pursuit of Happiness" for Locke's original triad of "Life, Liberty, and Estate (Property)"?

Consider the broader moral connotations of Greek/Scottish eudaimonia (virtue & public flourishing) x narrow landed wealth.

Hobbesian Counter-Argument

How would Thomas Hobbes criticize the American colonists' decision to declare independence?

Hobbes argued that fracturing the "common power" dissolves civil society back into the brutal "war of every man against every man."
