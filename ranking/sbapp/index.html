🗣️ Smart Board Discussion Questions

  • The Hobbes vs. Locke Divide: "Raise your hand if you chose Hobbes for safety. Raise your hand if you chose Locke for freedom. Who wants to explain why safety must come before rights—or why rights are worth the risk?"
  • Plato's Expert Rule: "Plato believed ordinary citizens shouldn't vote because they can be tricked by smooth talkers. Do we use Plato's idea in America today? (Hint: Supreme Court, Federal Reserve, military commanders)."
  • Aristotle's Middle Class: "Aristotle said democracy only survives if there is a strong middle class. Why does a large gap between the super-rich and the poor create political instability?"
  • Montesquieu vs. Rousseau: "Montesquieu divided power among 3 branches to stop tyranny. Rousseau wanted everyone to vote directly on every law. What are the pros and cons of voting directly vs. electing representatives?"
  • Blackstone's Ratio: "Blackstone said: 'Better that ten guilty persons escape than that one innocent suffer.' Do you agree with this ratio? What happens if society flips that rule?"

📝 Sentence Starters (For Student Presentations)

Defending Tier Placement:
"I placed  [Thinker]  in [Tier] because their idea of  [Concept]  is essential to protecting American freedom today."
Highlighting a Major Trade-off:
"I ranked  [Thinker]  in B-Tier because while their idea of  [Strength]  is helpful, their system has a serious flaw:  [Weakness] ."
Debate Winner Defense:
"In the debate between  [Thinker A]  and  [Thinker B] , I chose  [Winner]  because our society needs  [Core Value]  more than  [Competing Value] ."
Civics Word Bank Clues:
• Rule of Law: Written laws apply to everyone, even leaders.
• Natural Rights: Life, Liberty, and Property (born with them).
• Separation of Powers: 3 branches check each other.
• Due Process: Fair court trial before punishment.
